## UPDATES

### 9/14/26

These renderings don't take into account my new screen size of 120". I'll likely need to move the L/R speakers outside of the screen, behind the wall to avoid too narrow of a front stage.

## Overview

Rough planned measurements:

* **Width**: 13' 4" (160 inches)
* **Depth**: 17' 6" (210 inches)
* **Height**: 8' 7" (103 inches)

Some rough renderings. This is assuming a 130" screen mounted on a crossbar. Black fabric panels will be created to surround the screen. Aiming for a 10 foot main listening position. Speakers placed in a 7.2.4 Atmos layout. Attempted to meet the optimal speaker angles as much as I could with the room. This will all likely change as construction progresses.
