## Audio

### Speakers

The **Ascend CMT-340SE2** will be great for the space at a really good price. The high sensitivity will allow for SLP to hit reference peaks at a 10' MLP easily at 100W. If I want to spend more money, I could consider going with the Ascend Sierra LX. They may be nicer if I was planning on some mixed usage with music, but I will primarily be using the space for movies/TV. I'd also need to get a beefier amplifier (like the NCx500 or PURIFI) in order to push them to reference levels at my MLP. Unless I went with the Sierra towers... Something to consider...

I really like the RSL Speedwoofers when compared to SVS. Just seems to get more bang for your buck when comparing to the SVS subwoofers at their MSRP. Initially I was planning on putting both subs behind the screen wall, but with my room size, that may cause a significant bass null at the MLP. So a better option would be to put 1 up front and 1 in the rear of the room. I could still put the front subwoofer behind the screen, but that 20" of space kind of limits me to just the RSL 10S MKII if I did that. I'm really leaning towards going dual **RSL Speedwoofer 12S**, especially with budget savings elsewhere.

For surrounds, I think the **Ascend HTM-200SE2** would pair nicely with the CMT-340SE2 fronts. They also a little over 6" deep, are sealed, and can be mounted nicely on the wall which is a plus.

Going with really expensive atmos speakers is less important I think, so I am looking at the **RSL C34E MKII** as in-ceiling options. Decent price, and have a 15° angle so I can point them a bit closer to the MLP.

Definitely going to pre-wire for 9.6 for any future ideas... (front height, center ceiling).

### Receiver/Amp

The Ascend CMD-340SE2 can easily hit reference peaks with the 105W from a Denon receiver. BUT since I am aiming for 11 powered channels, I would need an external amplifier anyways (unless I went with an 11 channel amp). Even though it may be overkill, I may go with the **Buckeye NCx500 3-channel** ($1650) amplifier. It's a really nice Class D amp (less heat, quieter) that will serve me well for a long time. If I wanted to save money, I could go with the **Emotiva BasX A3+ 3-channel** ($799), but its significantly bigger. Really torn here.

With the receiver, I am eyeing the **Denon X3900H**. Handles all the formats I care about, supports DIRAC ART, and has the necessary pre-outs for an amp. The Marantz looks nice, but this receiver will likely be hidden. Seems to be cheaper to get the X3900H + external amp rather than splurge on an 11-channel receiver.

## Projector

Going with the **JVC DLA-NZ500** (\$5999). Seems to be the better overall (without spending 15+ grand). Only 2000 lumens but should be fine in a light-controlled room. Also natively does 4k rather than relying on pixel shifting like some other options. Does not support Dolby Vision but can try and do LLDV with the Ugoos or utilize JVCs dynamic HDR tone mapping.

The JVC DLA-NZ700 (\$8999) isn't worth the price jump. The lumens are slightly higher and their is better color contrast. BUT -- you have to enable a cinematic filter to get the better contrast and you actually end up losing some brightness doing that (even more than the NZ500).

## Screen

From researching acoustically transparent screens, I'm in stuck a bit. I could go with the **SeymourAV Precision CenterStage XD H105 120"** screen ($1363) . Great screen, great frame build quality, great company. The only problem is the weave on the XD screen can be visible at 10 feet. I haven't seen an example so I don't know if I would be susceptible to it or not. Youthman on YouTube has a CenterStage XD and sits at 9 feet and doesn't mention seeing the weave. The alternative is the **XY Screens Sound Max 4k 120"** screen ($730). Similar acoustic properties, similar gain, but much smoother texture. From multiple reports, the texture is smooth up to 6 feet from the screen. I could move my viewing position up to 9 feet and increase my viewing angle for more immersion without having worry about seeing the weave like I would with the Seymour screen. The only hesitation I have is some QA concerns from reports on AVSforum (wrinkle, misaligned screw holes, etc.). But in terms of the fabric, it seems to be highly praised. It also is almost half the price of the Seymour screen.
