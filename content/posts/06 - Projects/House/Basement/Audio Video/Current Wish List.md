# Audio

## Speakers

The **Ascend CMT-340SE2** will be great for the space at a really good price. The high sensitivity will allow for SLP to hit reference peaks at a 10' MLP easily. Also, they would only need 100W of power to hit those peaks so I can skip out on an amplifier (for now...). If I want to spend more money, I could consider going with the Ascend Sierra LX and pairing those with an external amp (since they will need the extra power). The Sierras may be nicer if I was planning on some mixed usage with music, but I will primarily be using the space for movies/TV. Something to consider...

I really like the RSL Speedwoofers when compared to SVS. Just seems to get more bang for your buck when comparing to the SVS subwoofers at their MSRP. The **RSL 10S MKII** would fit behind the wall better, but I think the RSL 12S would pack a bigger punch. Would just have to give up a few more inches in the room to move the screen further away from the wall. Would be about a \$600 difference between the two options. Alternatively I could move the subwoofers outside of the screen wall (either front corners or front/back).

For surrounds, I think the **Ascend HTM-200SE2** would timbre match nicely with the CMT-340SE2 fronts. They also a little over 6" deep, are sealed, and can be mounted nicely on the wall which is a plus.

Going with really expensive atmos speakers is less important I think, so I am looking at the **RSL C34E MKII** as in-ceiling options. Decent price, and have a 15° angle so I can point them a bit closer to the MLP. Another thing worth considering is just putting more **HTM-200SE2** up high on the ceiling as height channels instead, but that's not exactly "Dolby spec" according to Dolby's diagrams.

## Receiver/Amp

For the Ascend CMT-340SE2, I won't necessarily need an external amp, since even the Denon X3900H can put out the 100W they need to hit reference peaks. BUT since I am aiming for 11 powered channels, I would need one (unless I went with an 11 channel amp). I think I will go with the **Buckeye NCx500 3-channel** amplifier (\$1650). Its a bit overkill for the Ascend CMTs, but opens up my options down the road if I go with less sensitive or more power-demanding speakers.

With the receiver, I am eyeing the **Denon X3900H**. Handles all the formats I care about, supports DIRAC ART, and has the necessary pre-outs for an amp. The Marantz looks nice, but this receiver will likely be hidden.

# Projector

Going with the **JVC DLA-NZ500** (\$6999). Seems to be the better overall (without spending 15+ grand). Only 2000 lumens but will work great in a light-controlled room. Also natively does 4k rather rely on pixel shifting like some other options. Does not support Dolby Vision but can try and do LLDV with the Ugoos or utilize JVCs dynamic HDR tone mapping.

# Screen

From researching acoustically transparent screens, I've settled on going with the  **SeymourAV Precision CenterStage XD H115** screen ($1535), a 131" diagonal. I don't know if I'll need a black backing layer or not (+$400).
