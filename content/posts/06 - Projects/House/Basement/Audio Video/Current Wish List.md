## Audio

### Speakers

The **Ascend CMT-340SE2** will be great for the space at a really good price. The high sensitivity will allow for SLP to hit reference peaks at a 10' MLP easily at 100W. If I want to spend more money, I could consider going with the Ascend Sierra LX. They may be nicer if I was planning on some mixed usage with music, but I will primarily be using the space for movies/TV. I'd also need to get a beefier amplifier (like the NCx500 or PURIFI) in order to push them to reference levels at my MLP. Unless I went with the Sierra towers... Something to consider...

I really like the RSL Speedwoofers when compared to SVS. Just seems to get more bang for your buck when comparing to the SVS subwoofers at their MSRP. Initially I was planning on putting both subs behind the screen wall, but with my room size, that may cause a significant bass null at the MLP. So a better option would be to put 1 up front and 1 in the rear of the room. I could still put the front subwoofer behind the screen, but that 20" of space kind of limits me to just the **RSL 10S MKII** if I did that. The **RSL 12S** looks significantly better, but is a big boy. If I went that route, I'd need to put it in the corner in front of the screen wall.

For surrounds, I think the **Ascend HTM-200SE2** would timbre match nicely with the CMT-340SE2 fronts. They also a little over 6" deep, are sealed, and can be mounted nicely on the wall which is a plus.

Going with really expensive atmos speakers is less important I think, so I am looking at the **RSL C34E MKII** as in-ceiling options. Decent price, and have a 15° angle so I can point them a bit closer to the MLP. Another thing worth considering is just putting more **HTM-200SE2** up high on the ceiling as height channels instead, but that's not exactly "Dolby spec" according to Dolby's diagrams.

### Receiver/Amp

For the Ascend CMT-340SE2, I won't necessarily need an external amp, since even the Denon X3900H can put out the 100W they need to hit reference peaks. BUT since I am aiming for 11 powered channels, I would need one (unless I went with an 11 channel amp). In order to save money to spend more on subwoofers, I think I'll go with the **Emotiva BasX A3+ 3-channel** ($799). That will push 140W/channel which is plenty for the LCR speakers. Saves me $1000 rather than going with a Buckeye amp. If I did decide to change my LCR plans, I'd probably need to upgrade to the **Buckeye NCx500 3-channel** (\$1650) though.

With the receiver, I am eyeing the **Denon X3900H**. Handles all the formats I care about, supports DIRAC ART, and has the necessary pre-outs for an amp. The Marantz looks nice, but this receiver will likely be hidden. Seems to be cheaper to get the X3900H + external amp rather than splurge on an 11-channel receiver.

## Projector

Going with the **JVC DLA-NZ500** (\$6999). Seems to be the better overall (without spending 15+ grand). Only 2000 lumens but will work great in a light-controlled room. Also natively does 4k rather rely on pixel shifting like some other options. Does not support Dolby Vision but can try and do LLDV with the Ugoos or utilize JVCs dynamic HDR tone mapping.

## Screen

From researching acoustically transparent screens, I've settled on going with the **SeymourAV Precision CenterStage XD H115** screen ($1535), a 131" diagonal. I don't know if I'll need a black backing layer or not (+$400).
