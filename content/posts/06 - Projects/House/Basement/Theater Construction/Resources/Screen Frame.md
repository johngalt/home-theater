# Ideal

Taken from BIGmouthinDC (thread [here](https://www.avsforum.com/threads/minimalist-approach-to-screen-wall.837848/)). Build a basic frame (include a bottom horizontal bar too, probably). This will support the screen.\
![Pasted image 20260909195251.png](/ob/99%20-%20Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260909195251.png)

Then for the gaps on the sides/top/bottom, can build small frames with fabric:\
![Pasted image 20260909195612.png](/ob/99%20-%20Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260909195612.png)

In these builds he just friction fits the panels around the screen. Maybe a small furrowing side on the left/right. Maybe some velcro to hold them in place.
