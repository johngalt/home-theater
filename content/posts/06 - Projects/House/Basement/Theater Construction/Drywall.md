# Goal

Will use double 5/8" drywall with Green Glue (dampening compound) between the two layers on the walls and ceiling. This will provide mass and dampening for sound isolation (along with the decoupled framing of the room).  Diagram from the manufacturer showing the use on walls (would be the same for the ceiling):\

# Notes

## Green Glue

Plan on using double 5/8" gypsum with Green Glue (dapmening compound) between the two layers. See [here](https://shop.soundproofingcompany.com/products/green-glue-5-gallon-pail?variant=33152718110855). Will need 58OZ per 4x8 sheet, or "1-2 Speedload loads" per sheet:

1. Install in a random pattern to the back of drywall.
2. Press the board into place.
3. Screw board to framing following local building codes.\
   When hanging drywall, do so in a staggered fashion so seams from both layers don't line up.

## Acoustic Sealing

Acoustic sealing between seams on FIRST layer of drywall. Seams between drywall and to the floor/ceiling. Second layer of drywall can be mudded like normal.\
